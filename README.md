# CopyMe

Save links and text, then paste them wherever you are typing in Chrome.

---

## What it does

- Save links and snippets in the extension.
- Click inside any typing box — a social post, a message, Google Docs, Google Sheets, or any website form.
- Open CopyMe and tap a saved card. The text is pasted at your typing cursor.

---

## How to install

1. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions`
2. Turn on **Developer mode** (top right)
3. Click **Load unpacked** and choose this folder:
   `/Users/sndp/Documents/CopyMe`
4. Pin CopyMe to the Chrome toolbar

After any update, click **Reload** on the CopyMe card in `chrome://extensions`, then refresh the webpage you will type in.

---

## How to paste at your cursor

1. Click where you want to type, so your keyboard is active there.
2. Click the **CopyMe** icon in the Chrome toolbar. A panel appears on the page.
3. Tap a saved card (link or text).
4. The clip is inserted at that cursor. You can keep typing.

To save something new, type or paste it in the box at the top of the panel, or use **+ Current Tab**.

Click the CopyMe icon again, or press Escape, to close the panel.

Chrome blocks extensions on some built-in pages such as `chrome://extensions` and the Chrome Web Store.
